# DiscoverSet Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 7 August 2026
**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

DiscoverSet is an app for identifying tracks played at DJ sets and raves. You upload a short
clip, and other people help you work out what the track is.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it.
We have tried to write it in plain language rather than legal boilerplate. If anything here
is unclear, email us and we will explain it.

**Who is responsible for your data.** DiscoverSet is operated by Ewan Nicol, based in the
United Kingdom. Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation — and the EU GDPR, where
you are in the EEA — we are the "data controller" for the information described here.

**How to reach us.** ewan.nicol55@gmail.com

| | |
|---|---|
| **We collect** | Your email, your profile, the clips you upload, and what you do in the app |

## What we collect, and why

### Information you give us

**Account information.** An email address and a password, handled by our hosting provider
Supabase. We never see or store your password in a readable form. We need this to create
your account and let you sign back in.

**Profile information.** A username, and optionally a display name, a short bio, and an
avatar image. Your username is required because content in DiscoverSet is attributed — people
need to know who uploaded a clip and who suggested a track.

**Clips and the information attached to them.** The video itself, a thumbnail generated
from it, and the details you enter: artist name, venue, event date, caption, and the genre
community you post to.

**Please read this part before you upload.** A clip carries more than music. Taken
together, a venue, a date and a video are a record that a particular person was at a
particular place on a particular night. That may include people in the background who did
not choose to be filmed. Think about that before uploading, and do not upload clips from
places where filming was not allowed.

**Your contributions to the community.** Track suggestions, votes on other people's
suggestions, and likes.

### Information created as you use DiscoverSet

**Your social graph.** Which genre communities you follow, your friendships, and any accounts
you have blocked.

**Reports you file.** If you report a clip or a suggestion, we store what you reported, the
reason you picked, any note you added, and who you are. Reports are visible to us and to you,
never to the person reported.

**Your points.** DiscoverSet awards points for helping identify tracks. We keep a record of
each award — what it was for and when — so that your total is auditable and you can see
your own history.

**Timestamps.** Everything you create is stamped with when it was created.

### Information we do not collect

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, social
login providers, or cookies for tracking. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do
not track you across other apps or websites. We do not collect your location — the venue on
a clip is text you typed, not a GPS reading. We do not ask for your phone number, date of
birth, or payment details, because nothing in DiscoverSet costs money.

## Device permissions

DiscoverSet asks for three permissions on your device. Each is used only for the stated purpose,
and you can refuse or revoke any of them in your device settings — the app still works,
you just cannot upload.

- **Camera** — to record a clip.
- **Microphone** — to capture audio while recording. Without it a clip cannot be identified.
- **Photo library** — to let you choose a video you already recorded. We read only the
  file you pick. We do not scan, index, or upload the rest of your library.

## What is public and what is not

This section matters more than any other in this policy, and it is written directly.

**Public — visible to anyone, including people with no DiscoverSet account:**

- Your username, display name, bio and avatar
- Every clip you upload, including its video file, thumbnail, artist, venue, event date
  and caption
- Every track suggestion you post and every vote you cast
- Every clip you like
- Your points total and your points history
- Which genre communities you follow

**Private:**

- Your email address and password
- Your friendships, including pending requests. Only the two people involved can see that
  a friend request exists.
- Who you have blocked. The person you blocked is not told, and cannot see the list.
- Reports you file. The person you reported is not told who reported them.

**One specific technical point you should understand.** Clip videos, thumbnails and avatar
images are stored in public storage and served over ordinary web links. Anyone who has one
of those links can open it directly in a browser without a DiscoverSet account. Deleting a clip
removes the link, but if someone has already downloaded or shared the file, we cannot pull
that copy back. Treat anything you upload as public and permanent.

## Our legal bases for using your data

Under GDPR we need a lawful basis for each use. Ours are:

| What we do | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Run your account, show your content, deliver the core features | Performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, act on reports, moderate content | Our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — running a community that is not hostile to be in |
| Keep records we are legally required to keep, respond to lawful requests | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |

We do not rely on consent for anything today, because we do nothing that requires it. If that
changes — see **Advertising** below — we will ask for your consent first.

## Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. It goes to
three sorts of recipient only.

**Other DiscoverSet users, and the public.** As set out in the section above.

**Service providers who run our infrastructure.** These act on our instructions as
"processors" and cannot use your data for their own purposes:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, file storage and authentication. Everything described
  in this policy lives on Supabase infrastructure. Our project is hosted in Supabase's
  West Europe (London) region.
- **Apple and Google** — distribute the app and tell us aggregate download and crash
  numbers. Their handling of your data as an App Store or Play Store customer is governed
  by their own privacy policies, not this one.

**Authorities, where we are legally required to.** If we receive a valid legal request we
may have to disclose information. Where we are permitted to tell you, we will.

**International transfers.** Your data is stored in Supabase's West Europe (London) region,
in the United Kingdom. Supabase is a US company and its staff may access infrastructure from
outside your country for support and maintenance. Where that involves a transfer out of the
UK/EEA it is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK
International Data Transfer Addendum.

## Advertising

**DiscoverSet does not show ads today.** There is no advertising SDK in the app, no ad
identifier is read, and no data is shared with any advertising network.

We may introduce advertising in future — it is a plausible way to cover hosting costs for a
free app. If we do, the change will be announced rather than left for you to discover. Before
any advertising launches we will:

1. Update this policy and change the "last updated" date, describing exactly which
   advertising provider we use and what it receives.
2. Notify you in the app, rather than relying on you to re-read this page.
3. **Ask for your consent before any personalised advertising** — including Apple's
   App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS, and a consent choice for users in the UK and
   EEA. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised and no
   advertising identifier will be shared.
4. Update our App Store and Play Store privacy disclosures to match.

Until all of that has happened, nothing in this section applies and no advertising data is
being collected.

## How long we keep things

- **While your account is open**, we keep your account, profile and content so the app
  works.
- **When you delete your account**, everything goes with it: your profile, clips, suggestions,
  votes, likes, follows, friendships, points records, your blocked list, and any reports you
  filed or that were filed about you. The video and image files are removed from storage. There
  is no soft delete, no hidden copy, and no waiting period.
- **Encrypted backups** may contain your data for up to 30 days after deletion, after which
  they age out. We do not restore backups to recover deleted accounts.
- **Reports** are kept while the reported content exists, so that a pattern across several
  reports is visible to us. If the content or the account behind it is deleted, the reports
  about it go too.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** anything inaccurate — most of it you can edit yourself in the app
- **Delete** your data (the "right to erasure")
- **Take your data with you** in a portable, machine-readable format
- **Object to** or **restrict** processing we base on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent**, for anything we ever ask consent for
- **Complain** to a data protection authority. You can go to the regulator in your own
  country at any time; you do not have to raise it with us first, though we would like the
  chance to fix it.

To exercise any of these, email ewan.nicol55@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, as
GDPR requires. We will not charge you, and you will not receive a worse service for having
asked.

**Deleting your account.** You can do this yourself, in the app: **Settings → Delete account**.
It takes effect immediately, we do not ask you why, and there is nothing to wait for. If you
cannot sign in, email ewan.nicol55@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will do it
for you.

## Security

Your password is hashed, never stored in readable form. All traffic between the app and our
servers is encrypted in transit, and data is encrypted at rest. Access to our database is
governed by row-level security rules, so the app can only read what this policy says it can.
Administrative access is limited to Ewan Nicol.

No service can promise it will never be breached. If a breach affects your personal data
and creates a real risk to you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator within 72 hours
of becoming aware, as the law requires.

## Children

DiscoverSet is not for children. You must be **16 or older** to create an account. The app is
about club and festival music and the content people post reflects that.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it. If you are a
parent or guardian and believe your child has an account, email ewan.nicol55@gmail.com and we
will remove it.
